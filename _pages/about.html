About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Kentucky. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from BRAC University in 2021, graduating with the highest distinction. My research focuses on communication-efficient multimodal federated learning: training models collaboratively across distributed devices that hold different types of data, without sharing the raw data, while compressing the model updates they exchange to reduce communication costs.

Before starting my PhD, I was a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I taught courses including Programming Language I, Data Structures, Digital Logic Design, and Database Systems.
