About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Kentucky, where I have been a Graduate Teaching Assistant since Fall 2025 and was previously a Graduate Research Assistant (Fall 2024 - Summer 2025) working on federated learning, algorithms, databases, and networking. I also serve as the GSC Representative for the Association for Data and Computation (ADC) and as Sports Officer for the Bangladeshi Student Association at the University of Kentucky (BSAUK).

Before starting my PhD, I was a Lecturer (and previously Adjunct Lecturer and Undergraduate Teaching Assistant) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in Dhaka, Bangladesh, where I taught courses including Programming Language I, Data Structures, Digital Logic Design, and Database Systems. I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University in 2021, graduating with the highest distinction.

My undergraduate thesis and subsequent publications focused on deep learning approaches for COVID-19 detection from chest X-ray and CT scan imaging.
